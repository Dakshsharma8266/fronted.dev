Introduction

Agra, home to the Taj Mahal, is one of India's most historic cities with rich Mughal heritage.

Heritage

The city boasts architectural marvels like Agra Fort and Fatehpur Sikri that depict Mughal grandeur.

Culture

Agra’s culture blends art, music, cuisine, and crafts, offering a glimpse of India’s royal past.
