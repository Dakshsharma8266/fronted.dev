Top 3 Travel Destinations

1. Paris

Paris is known for art, fashion, and the Eiffel Tower.

“A city of light and romance.” — Traveler A

2. Kyoto

Kyoto offers historic temples and serene gardens.

“Peaceful and steeped in tradition.” — Traveler B

3. New Zealand

New Zealand is famous for dramatic landscapes and outdoor adventures.

“A paradise for nature lovers.” — Traveler C

Travel Tips

  • Carry photocopies of your passport.
  • Learn basic local phrases.
  • Book accommodations in advance during peak season.
